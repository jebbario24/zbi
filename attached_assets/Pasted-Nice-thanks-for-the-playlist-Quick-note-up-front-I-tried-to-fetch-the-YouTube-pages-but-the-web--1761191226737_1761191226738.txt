# 3) Marketing & Growth layer (how to make Uber paid tactics free inside your product)

You want to give restaurants free marketing tools Uber charges for — implement them as built-in features that consume platform “credits” or are unlimited (your choice). Below are recommended free features and how they function.

A. Free Boosts (Featured Placement)

* Each restaurant gets a small daily boost credit (e.g., 1 free boost/day). Boost rotates restaurants into a "Featured" slot in the consumer app for X minutes.
* Implementation: a scheduler rotates restaurants with active boosts; boosts are controlled in Marketing → Boosts.
* Data: boost_credits table (tenant_id, credits_balance, last_reset_date)

B. Automated Smart Promos (auto discounts)

* Admin can enable “Auto-offers” with rules (off-peak discount, slow day discount, high-cart discount).
* System can auto-apply if order meets criteria; restaurants can opt out of auto application.
* Track lift with PromoPerformance events.

C. Loyalty & Tiers (free)

* Points per currency spent; redeemable as fixed value or % off.
* Tiers (Bronze/Silver/Gold) provide automatic micro-boosts and featured rotation.
* Data: loyalty_accounts, loyalty_points_transactions, loyalty_tiers.

D. Free Listing Analytics

* Built in at no extra cost; show items with highest margin, conversion funnel, and promo ROI.

E. Automated Campaigns (prebuilt templates)

* Templates: Welcome discount, Reactivation (30 days inactive), Birthday coupon, Abandoned cart push, Post-order cross-sell.
* Restaurants can enable templates and customize texts and discounts.
* Campaign engine uses customer segmentation (order history) and a scheduler.

F. Referral & Social Share

* Quick QR/post templates for Instagram, Tiktok with UGC prompts. Offer automatic first-order referral credits.

G. Smart Recommendations / Upsells

* When building an item, allow admin to add “Smart Pairing” rules. On cart, show one-line suggestion + single-tap add.

H. A/B Promo Testing Dashboard

* Give restaurants the ability to create two promo variants, run for a window, and view which converts better.
